About DSTA

DSTA Solutions is a data operations and business intelligence company helping businesses turn scattered, inconsistent data into structured and reliable business information. From data cleaning and recurring MIS reporting to dashboards, analytics and process automation, we support businesses in building better reporting workflows and making informed decisions.

Our Services

📊 Data Operations

Data cleaning, consolidation, validation and structured data management to keep business information organised and reliable.

📈 MIS & Reporting

Daily, weekly and monthly business reports, KPI tracking, sales analysis and management reporting to support better decisions.

📉 Business Intelligence & Dashboards

Interactive dashboards, business performance analysis and visual reporting using tools such as Excel and Power BI.

⚙️ Reporting Process Automation

Streamlining repetitive reporting tasks and improving reporting workflows through Excel, Power Query and other suitable tools.

🧹 Data Cleaning & Validation

Identifying duplicates, missing values, inconsistencies and formatting issues to prepare data for dependable analysis.

👥 Dedicated Resource

Dedicated or shared data and reporting professionals for recurring business operations, MIS preparation and ongoing reporting requirements, based on agreed scope and availability.
